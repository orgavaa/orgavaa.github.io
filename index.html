Research

Tools and experiments for measuring biology, learning from biological data and evaluating what models can reliably predict.

Independent, open-source work. Not peer-reviewed.

  • COMPASS: Designing CRISPR diagnostics

    A computational platform for multiplexed diagnostic panels targeting drug-resistant tuberculosis. Connects known resistance markers to candidate assays and models of diagnostic activity. Experimental validation pending.

    2026PhD proposal @ ETHCodePlatform

  • B-JEPA: Learning from bacterial genomes

    Self-supervised representations of bacterial DNA, combining joint-embedding prediction with masked-token learning. Early weights are available; downstream evaluation is pending.

    2026CodeWeights

  • GlycoQuant: Connecting the cell surface to cell mechanics

    Image-analysis software relating the glycocalyx to cellular responses to force. Turns fluorescence images into per-cell measurements of surface organisation, signalling and morphology.

    2026Code

  • When do protein language models capture antibiotic resistance?

    A retrospective analysis of 81 resistance mutations across four pathogens. Examines when protein-model scores reflect clinical prevalence, and where resistance mechanisms limit their usefulness.

    2026Code

  • Does 3D structure improve antibody-binding predictions?

    Interface graph neural networks against simpler baselines on AB-Bind. On the test split, the GNN has lower absolute error than gradient boosting but explains less variance, so the benefit of structural modelling remains unresolved.

    2025Code
